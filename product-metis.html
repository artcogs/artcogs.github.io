METIS is a multi-agent crowd simulation technology developed through ArtCogS research, enabling virtual experiments with pedestrian movement, evacuation and crowd management. Its development spans configurable indoor simulations, AI-assisted crowd understanding and large-scale scenarios in 3D heritage environments.

What Is METIS?

METIS brings together configurable virtual environments and agent-based behaviour models to investigate how crowds move through buildings, public spaces and complex urban areas. Researchers and stakeholders can explore alternative conditions before interventions are implemented in the real world.

What started as an accessible crisis-simulation environment has grown through related research projects into a broader family of crowd simulation capabilities, including analysis of evacuation behaviour, AI-based crowd observations and integration with Digital Twin workflows.

Capabilities

Multi-Agent Crowds

Model distinct groups of virtual pedestrians and their interactions with spatial environments.

Evacuation Scenarios

Examine safe routes, movement constraints and response to simulated hazards.

Configurable 3D Environments

Experiment with buildings and larger outdoor spaces under different layouts and conditions.

Behavioural Variety

Represent ordinary movement, guided groups, exploration and evacuation in relevant service configurations.

Data and Analytics

Collect agent trajectories and simulation summaries for scenario comparison and further analysis.

Integration Potential

ARTEMIS work develops a JSON-driven, REST-accessible simulation service for Digital Twin integration.

Capabilities come from successive METIS-related research developments. Availability depends on the version, configuration and deployment; the page does not imply all features are currently packaged together in one released application.
