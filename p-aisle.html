AISLE is a prohect focusing on developing personalised skills assessment and guidance platform. It combines adaptive questionnaires, intelligent evaluation of open-ended responses and tailored recommendations to help people understand their skills and identify opportunities for development.

The Challenge

Traditional skills assessments often rely on fixed questionnaires and closed-form answers. They can struggle to reflect the different backgrounds, professional goals and learning needs of individual users, or to interpret the reasoning expressed in open-ended responses.

AISLE explores a more adaptive approach: using specialised AI agents to tailor assessment activities to each profile, analyse the supplied answers and translate the findings into meaningful guidance for skills development.

Planned Core Capabilities

Personalised Assessment

Adapt questions to a user's background, learning needs and professional aspirations.

Open-Ended Responses

Assess explanations, practical answers and scenario-based responses beyond fixed-choice tests.

Skill Gap Analysis

Identify strengths and potential gaps against the skills relevant to an assessment context.

Individual Guidance

Generate targeted suggestions for further learning and professional development.

Agentic AI

Distribute profiling, generation, evaluation and guidance across specialised AI components.

Web-Based Access

Provide a unified interface for assessment activities and reviewing their results.

Four Specialised AI Agents

The proposed architecture is organised around four cooperating agents, each addressing one stage of the assessment process.

1

Profile Analyzer

Interprets the user's profile and learning or professional objectives to establish the assessment context.

2

Questionnaire Generator

Creates personalised questions and tasks aligned with the profile and relevant skills.

3

Response Evaluator

Examines users' responses, including open text, and produces assessment findings.

4

Insight Agent

Transforms assessment results into understandable insights and recommendations for development.

How AISLE Is Designed to Work

1

Build a Profile

The user provides their background and objectives.

2

Generate an Assessment

The platform selects and creates context-relevant questions.

3

Evaluate Responses

Specialised models analyse answers and relevant evidence.

4

Receive Guidance

Results highlight strengths, gaps and possible next steps.

The intended information flows are Profile Responses → Profile Analyzer → Questionnaire Generator and Assessment Responses → Response Evaluator → Insight Agent → Results & Recommendations.

Technology and Research Direction

AISLE is designed around Large Language Models (LLMs), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and modular Agentic AI services. The proposed web platform communicates with the specialised agents through APIs, enabling components to be refined or extended independently.

Ongoing research and engineering priorities include the quality and consistency of AI-generated assessments, understandable feedback, scalability, privacy-aware data handling and validation with users and stakeholders.

Development status: This page describes the project vision and planned capabilities, not a publicly available or validated assessment service. AI-generated skills evaluations are intended to support human judgement rather than replace it in high-stakes decisions.
